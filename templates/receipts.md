# Receipts (sidecar — NOT prose)

Park **all** URLs, verbatim quotes, disputed figures, confidence labels, and
“allegation vs settled fact” notes here. `draft_clean.md` / VO must not perform
verification, soft-hedge theater, or competing tallies (rules M3 / M5).

Copy this file into `<run_dir>/receipts.md` at run init, then fill blocks.
Do not invent claim IDs in the publishable prose (unless `citation_mode` requires markers).

Status values: `VERIFIED` | `DISPUTED` | `UNVERIFIED` | `ALLEGATION`

```markdown
### [R01] Short claim summary
- **Status:** VERIFIED
- **Language:** EN | JA | other (optional)
- **Source:** Outlet / Author (Date) — https://...
- **Facts / Numbers:** 1989, $4.2 million, 55%
- **Verbatim Quote:** "exact quote if used in prose or needed for audit"
- **Caveat:** conflicting tallies, hedges, pleaded-only claims — stay HERE
```
